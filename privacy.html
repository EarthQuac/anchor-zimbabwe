Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Anchor Zimbabwe respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website or contact us, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Anchor is the business brand responsible for this website and for handling enquiries sent to us through it.

Contact: anchorstudio.digital@gmail.com
Website: zw.anchorstuds.com
Location: Zimbabwe

2. Information we collect

If you submit the project enquiry form, we may collect the information you choose to provide:

  • Your name
  • Your business name
  • Your business category
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number and country code
  • Your email address, if you provide one
  • Your project description, quote request or mockup request

You can also contact us directly by email or WhatsApp. In that case, we receive the information you choose to include in your message and the contact details associated with that service.

When you visit the website, the hosting and security services that deliver it may process basic technical information, such as an IP address, browser details, request time and security events, to operate, maintain and protect the website.

3. Why we use this information

We use enquiry information to respond to you, understand your requirements, prepare estimates, discuss and deliver requested work, provide support, and keep appropriate business records. We use technical information where needed to maintain website availability, diagnose errors and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use enquiry details for unrelated advertising or send marketing messages just because you contacted us.

4. Service providers and sharing

Some information is handled by service providers that help operate the website and communicate with you:

  • Formspree: processes submissions made through the website enquiry form.
  • Cloudflare: provides website hosting and related delivery or security services.
  • WhatsApp and LinkedIn: if you choose to use these links, you interact with those services under their own privacy terms.
  • Email providers: may process messages sent to or from our business email account.

These providers may process information on systems located outside Zimbabwe. Their handling of information is also subject to their own policies and applicable requirements. We do not disclose enquiry information to unrelated parties except where needed to provide a requested service, protect the website or our rights, or comply with the law.

5. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and project information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a potential or active project, provide support, maintain business records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. The period may depend on the nature of the enquiry and any work agreed.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No website, email service or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

At the date of this policy, the main Anchor website does not intentionally set or read cookies for analytics, advertising, visitor profiling or marketing. The separate project demos use browser storage for their own functions: the inventory demo stores product entries and a theme preference in local storage; the quote and invoice demo stores a theme preference in local storage and a temporary export count in session storage. The quote details you enter are used to generate the on-screen preview and are not sent to Anchor or saved as quote records by Anchor. This browser storage is not used by Anchor to track visitors or build advertising profiles. Data stored by a demo remains in your browser on that device unless you clear the relevant site data.

Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms, including cookies where applicable, to deliver the website, maintain security or protect against abusive traffic. These are controlled by the relevant provider and are not used by Anchor to build advertising profiles. If you follow a link to an external service such as WhatsApp or LinkedIn, that service may use its own cookies and technologies under its own privacy policy.

The browser storage described above supports demo features and is not used for analytics or advertising. Anchor does not currently use non-essential analytics or advertising cookies on this website. If we add analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking later, we will update this policy and provide any consent or choices required by applicable law before enabling it.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to explain how we use your personal information, provide access to information we hold about you, correct inaccurate information, or delete information where deletion is appropriate. You may also object to certain processing. To make a request, email anchorstudio.digital@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy Request”. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Providing the information marked as required in the enquiry form is necessary for us to respond to that enquiry. If you do not provide it, we may be unable to contact you or assess your request.

9. Children

This website and our business services are intended for businesses and people able to make enquiries on their behalf. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

11. Zimbabwean law

We aim to handle personal information in a fair and responsible way, having regard to applicable Zimbabwean data protection law, including the Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]. This policy describes our current website practices and is not a legal opinion or a guarantee of regulatory compliance.

Questions about privacy? Email anchorstudio.digital@gmail.com.
