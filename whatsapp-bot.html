WHATSAPP AUTOMATION

Give Customers Quick Answers on WhatsApp

Customers often message with the same questions about prices, opening hours, services and how to place an order. A WhatsApp bot can handle those first steps, guide people to the information they need and pass the conversation to you when a person is needed.

Types of WhatsApp Bots

Different businesses need different levels of automation. These are the main types we can configure, from straightforward menus to more involved workflows.

1. FAQ and information bot

From $150 · Usually 2-3 working days

Welcomes customers and answers common questions about services, prices, opening hours, location and business policies. It can offer a simple menu and pass customers to a person when needed.

2. Customer enquiry and lead capture bot

From $250 · Usually 3-5 working days

Guides potential customers through a few questions, collects details such as their name and what they need, then sends the enquiry to the business for follow-up. Useful for service businesses and quote requests.

3. Order-taking bot

From $350 · Usually 5-7 working days

Shows an agreed product or service menu, collects item choices and quantities, captures customer and delivery details, and sends an order summary to your team. More complex catalogues or system connections are quoted separately.

4. Appointment and booking bot

Quoted based on booking requirements

Collects a customer's preferred service, date and time, then forwards the request or connects to an agreed calendar or booking system. Live availability checks and calendar integrations add scope.

5. AI-assisted customer support bot

From $500 · Scoped individually

Can use an approved knowledge source to respond to a wider range of customer questions, with clear limits and a handover path. It needs additional setup, testing and ongoing provider costs, so suitability and pricing are confirmed after a discussion.

Prices are starting estimates for Anchor's setup work, not WhatsApp or provider fees. Final pricing depends on the number of conversation paths, integrations, content and platform requirements. Hosting, API usage and other third-party charges are separate.

What's included

  • A welcome message that explains the options available
  • Automated answers to a defined set of common questions
  • A simple menu to help customers find prices, services or business information
  • Enquiry capture, where customers can leave the details you need to follow up
  • A clear route for handing the conversation over to you or your staff
  • Setup, testing and a walkthrough of how the bot works

Who this is for

Shops, restaurants, service providers and growing businesses that receive regular WhatsApp enquiries and want customers to find basic information without waiting for a manual reply.

How setup works

  1. We discuss the questions customers ask and what information they need
  2. We agree on the bot's menu, answers and handover points
  3. We confirm the suitable WhatsApp setup and any third-party requirements before work begins
  4. We configure and test the conversation using realistic customer questions
  5. We hand it over with guidance on managing the setup

Common questions

Does the bot answer every message?

No. The basic setup follows the agreed questions and conversation paths. Unrecognised questions or requests that need judgement can be passed to a person.

Is this the same as an AI chatbot?

No. The starting package is a structured bot with predefined answers and menus. AI features or more advanced integrations can be scoped separately if they are suitable for your business.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business Platform?

Some automated integrations require the official WhatsApp Business Platform and an eligible business setup. We will check the requirements with you before confirming the project. Any provider, hosting or platform charges are separate from Anchor's setup fee.

How long does it take?

A straightforward FAQ bot usually takes around 2-3 working days once the content, access and required platform setup are ready. Order-taking flows and external integrations take longer and receive a separate quote.

Can it take orders?

It can be extended to collect order details and send them to your team or an agreed system. That is a larger scope than the basic FAQ setup, so we quote it separately.
