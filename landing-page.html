LANDING PAGE DESIGN

A Focused Page for One Clear Offer

A landing page gives a specific product, service, campaign or offer a clear place to send potential customers. Instead of spreading attention across a large website, it presents the important details and guides visitors towards one next step.

What's included

  • Custom page design built around one offer or campaign
  • Responsive layout for phones, tablets and desktops
  • Clear sections for the offer, benefits, details and call to action
  • WhatsApp or enquiry contact link
  • Basic on-page SEO title and description
  • Launch support and a final review

Who this is for

Businesses promoting one service, product, event or campaign, or those who need a simple professional web presence before investing in a larger website.

How the project works

  1. We discuss the offer, target audience and action you want visitors to take
  2. We organise the content into a clear page structure
  3. We design and build the page for mobile and desktop
  4. We add the agreed contact or enquiry action
  5. We review the page with you and publish it once approved

Common questions

How is a landing page different from a full website?

A landing page is focused on one offer and one main action. A full business website usually has several pages covering the wider company, services and information.

Can I add a WhatsApp button?

Yes. We can include a direct WhatsApp link or an enquiry form, depending on how you want customers to contact you.

Can it be expanded later?

Yes. If your needs grow, the page can be used as a starting point for a larger business website. The extra work would be quoted separately.

Can you use my existing website or social pages?

Yes. We can use your existing branding, copy and images, then agree what needs to be created or improved for the project.

Are domain and hosting included?

The starting price covers the defined design and build scope. Domain registration, paid hosting, premium assets and other third-party costs are discussed separately where needed.

Can I request extra changes?

Yes. The included revision scope is agreed before work starts. Additional pages, features or substantial changes may require a revised quote.
