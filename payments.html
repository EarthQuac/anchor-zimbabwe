PAYMENTS · ZIMBABWE

Pay for Your Project with EcoCash

We want getting started with your website or digital solution to be straightforward. For clients in Zimbabwe, EcoCash is our available local payment method for now.

How to pay

  1. Contact Anchor and tell us which service or project you would like to proceed with.
  2. We’ll send you a written quote with the agreed price, payment schedule and verified EcoCash payment details.
  3. Before sending money, check that the recipient name and amount match the details we gave you.
  4. After paying, send us your payment confirmation on WhatsApp and include your project or quote reference.

Payment information

Accepted method

EcoCash (Zimbabwe). Other payment methods are not currently listed.

Where do I get the EcoCash number?

Request it directly from Anchor after your quote is confirmed. We share the correct number and recipient name with you privately; please don’t use a number from an unverified message or third-party source.

How much should I pay?

Your quote will confirm the amount due, any deposit and when the remaining balance is payable. Please wait for those details before making a payment.
