SOUND FAMILIAR?

Everyday business challenges that deserve better solutions.

Running a business in Zimbabwe takes resourcefulness. The right digital tools can make everyday work clearer, faster and easier to manage.

01

Answering the same questions every day?

Customers repeatedly ask for prices, available stock, directions or ways to order, taking time away from other work.

02

Stock says one thing. The shelf says another.

When stock records are updated by hand, it is harder to spot shortages, track sales and know what needs restocking.

03

Orders get lost between WhatsApp, calls, and notebooks

Orders spread across WhatsApp messages, phone calls and paper notes are difficult to track and follow up.

04

Important information lives with one person

When key information sits with one person, even routine tasks can be delayed when they are away.

05

Too much time goes into reports and admin

Repeated data entry and manual reporting take time that could be spent serving customers and running the business.

06

Customers cannot quickly understand what you offer

If people cannot easily find your business or understand what you offer online, they may move on to another option.
